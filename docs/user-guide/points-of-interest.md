# Points of interest

Points of interest are square zones on the map. The selected one defines the download area and the area for model runs. Study areas, by contrast, only limit searches.

```{note}
Points of interest live in memory. Reloading the page removes them.
```

## Add a point

1. Open **Points of interest** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Mark point**.
3. Click the map once. A 10 km square appears and becomes selected.

Press `Esc` to cancel placing. Marking a point and drawing a study area are exclusive: starting one stops the other.

## Edit a point

- Drag the center to move the square.
- Drag a corner of the selected square to resize it. The side ranges from 1 to 100 km.
- In the sidebar list (name or "Point N" with coordinates), click a point to select it. The editor shows coordinates, **Range (side)** slider and number input, name, note, **Go to** and **Delete**.
- The round menu button at the top-right corner of the selected square offers **Delete point of interest**.

When zoomed out and the square becomes small on screen, it turns into a pin. Click the pin to zoom to the square.

## Download

**Download** is disabled when no point is selected. If study areas exist and the square lies fully outside them, a yellow warning reads "The point is outside the study area". The download still works.
